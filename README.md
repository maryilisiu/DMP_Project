
## 1. EchoFlutter 🦋

**Description:**  
EchoFlutter is a headband with flapping wings that react to sounds. Two servo motors are placed above the ears, each carrying a wing made of lightweight air-dry clay. The motors are controlled by an Arduino Uno, which processes audio signals from a MAX4466 microphone sensor. When sounds such as clapping or music are detected, the wings move up and down.

**Components:**
- Arduino Uno – 1
- SG90 micro servo motors – 2
- MAX4466 microphone sensor – 1
- 3-pin servo extension cables (50–100 cm) – 2
- Flexible silicone wires – As needed
- Braided cable sleeve – 1
- Regulated 5V servo power supply (around 2A capacity) – 1
- Suitable USB power source for Arduino – 1
- Headband + lightweight wings – 1 set
- Plastic Arduino enclosure with clothing clip – 1

## 2. BloomBot 🌸

**Description:**  
BloomBot is an interactive flower that opens its petals when someone approaches or when it detects enough light. An ultrasonic sensor detects nearby objects, while an LDR sensor measures the surrounding light intensity. A servo motor controls the opening and closing of the petals, and an RGB LED in the flower's center changes color depending on its state.

**Components:**
- Arduino Uno – 1
- SG90 servo motor – 1
- HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor – 1
- LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) – 1
- RGB LED – 1
- 220 Ω resistors – 3
- 10 kΩ resistor – 1
- External regulated 5V power supply – 1
- Jumper wires – As needed
- Paper / EVA foam – As needed
- Flower pot – 1
- Fishing line / mechanical linkage – As needed

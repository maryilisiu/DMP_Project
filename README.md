# DMP_Project
1.EchoFlutter: 
    A headband with flapping wings that react when sounds are detected.On the headband there will be 2 servo motors(right above      the ears) which will carry wings made of ligh dry clay. These 2 motors will be connected to a MAX4466 microphone sensor          which will trigger the motors to move in an up-down direction when detecting sounds(clapping, songs for example).
    Components:
            Arduino Uno	- 1
            SG90 micro servo motors	- 2
            MAX4466 microphone sensor	- 1
            3-pin servo extension cables (50–100 cm)	- 2
            Flexible silicone wires	- As needed
            Braided cable sleeve	- 1
            Regulated 5V servo power supply (around 2A capacity)	- 1
            Suitable USB power source for Arduino	- 1
            Headband + lightweight wings	- 1 set
            Plastic Arduino enclosure with clothing clip	- 1

2.BloomBot:
    BloomBot is be an interactive flower that opens its petals when someone approaches or when it detects enough light.
    Components:
        Arduino Uno – Main microcontroller.
        SG90 Servo Motor – Opens and closes the flower petals.
        HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor – Detects nearby people or objects.
        LDR (Light Dependent Resistor) – Measures surrounding light intensity.
        RGB LED – Placed in the center of the flower to create different lighting effects.
        3 × 220 Ω resistors – Limit current through the RGB LED's color channels.
        1 × 10 kΩ resistor – Used with the LDR to measure brightness.
        External regulated 5V power supply – Powers the servo motor.
        Jumper wires – Connect the components.
        Paper / EVA foam – Creates the flower petals.
        Flower pot – Holds the structure and hides the electronics.
        Fishing line / mechanical linkage – Connects the servo to the petals.
